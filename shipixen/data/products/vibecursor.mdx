## Product Overview

Bring your Mac screen to life!

VibeCursor adds stunning visual trails, mesmerizing click effects, and fluid animations to your cursor for a truly personalized macOS experience. Whether you're presenting, gaming, or just browsing, VibeCursor makes every movement feel smooth and responsive.

CORE FEATURES
- Customizable Visual Trails — 7 trail styles to match your vibe: Sparkle, Rainbow, Ghost, Lightning, Neon, Comet and Solid. Fine-tune color, width, length and transparency to match your workflow or wallpaper.
- Interactive Click Effects — Make every click feel alive with 7 styles: Ripple, Particle Burst, Emoji spray, Confetti, Fireworks, Starburst and Classic pulse. Customize color, size and opacity for perfect visual feedback.
- Immersive Audio Feedback — Satisfying click sounds that complement the visual experience, making every interaction feel more tactile.
- High Performance — Native SwiftUI rendering with GPU-accelerated canvas. Supports 120Hz/ProMotion for buttery-smooth animations with minimal CPU impact.
- Smart Idle Pause — Effects pause automatically when your cursor is still, and the master switch stops everything instantly. Focused when you need it, invisible when you don't.

DESIGNED FOR MAC
Native app optimized for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
