## Product Overview

BeginAnyway helps you actually start the hard morning task — not just wake up.

Set one important task the night before. When your alarm fires, BeginAnyway opens a Mission: take a quick photo proving you started, or hold a focused 2-minute timer. Build a streak for showing up. Keep history of what you started.

Built for iPhone on iOS 26. Alarms use Apple’s AlarmKit when available so they can break through Silent and Focus. If AlarmKit isn’t granted on your device yet, you can still use Missions from the app and Preview paths.
