## Product Overview

Your AI. Your phone. Your rules.

Pocket AI runs real open-source AI models directly on your iPhone. Download a model once, then chat completely offline: on the train, on a plane, in a tunnel, or anywhere with no signal. Your prompts and replies are generated on-device and stay on your phone.

CHAT THAT RUNS ON YOUR DEVICE
- 39 open LLMs to choose from: Qwen, Llama, Gemma, SmolLM, Ministral, Granite, Phi, DeepSeek distills and more.
- Real on-device inference powered by llama.cpp with Metal acceleration and live token streaming.
- Pick a model to match your iPhone: lightweight models fly on iPhone 12/13, larger models shine on the newest Pro devices.
- Once a model is downloaded, chat works with no internet, no account, and no sign-in.

TALK TO IT: ON-DEVICE VOICE (PRO)
- Speak instead of type. Voice input is transcribed locally with an on-device Whisper model, included in the one-time Pro unlock.
- Your audio is processed on your phone and is not uploaded to any server.

START EVERY DAY FOCUSED (PRO)
- Daily Brief turns the Calendar events and Reminders you choose into a focused, readable plan.
- Works with Apple Health: with your permission, Pocket AI uses HealthKit to read sleep, steps and workouts (read-only) and adds one line about your day to your brief. Health data stays on your device.
- Connect and refresh read-only sources from the new Sources screen. Source content is processed locally and never uploaded.
- Refresh whenever you want, or let Pocket AI prepare one brief per day on-device. If notifications are already allowed, Pocket AI can let you know when it is ready.
- Clear progress, numbered same-day briefs, and grounded summaries help you see what matters without losing the source context.

ONE-TAP WIDGETS
- Start a New Chat or Continue your latest conversation from redesigned Home Screen widgets.
- Quick actions also work across supported Lock Screen widget sizes.

MAKE IT YOURS
- 9 built-in personas, from ELI5 and Study Buddy to Code Helper, Writing Coach and Translator.
- 8 on-device tools: summarize, fix grammar, translate and more, powered by your local model.
- A 26-item marketplace of ready-made custom instructions, plus your own unlimited custom instructions.
- Daily challenges and streaks to keep exploring what your local models can do.
- Liquid-Glass design that feels right at home on iOS.

PRIVACY YOU CAN REASON ABOUT
- Your conversations, prompts and voice audio are processed on-device and never sent to an AI server. There is no cloud model answering you.
- Calendar events, Reminders and Apple Health data used for Daily Brief stay on your device and can be disconnected or deleted from Pocket AI at any time.
- Optional usage analytics sends content-free product interactions to PostHog and Firebase/Google Analytics to improve reliability. Disable it any time in Privacy settings. Chat text, prompts, voice audio, Calendar events, Reminders, Health data and Daily Brief content are never included.
- RevenueCat manages the optional Pro purchase using an anonymous app identifier and purchase history. Internet is also used for the model and voice-pack downloads you choose. Everyday chatting works in airplane mode.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL ONE-TIME PRO UNLOCK
- Free: 15 messages per day, 2 downloaded models, 3 personas and 3 tools.
- Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase. No subscription. It unlocks unlimited daily messages, all 39 models, every persona and tool, Daily Brief, on-device voice input, the full marketplace, and a deeper generation queue.

WHY POCKET AI
- Built for real iPhones, including older ones, not just the latest flagship.
- Open models, on your terms.
- The AI that still works when everything else doesn't.

Download a model and start chatting in under two minutes.
