## Product Overview

WhaleDeck is your all-in-one Docker environment manager. Monitor server performance, manage your containers, and use the built-in SSH terminal to run commands on your servers or inside Docker containers. Performance metrics, container logs, and actions across multiple servers are all in one place. Whether you’re just starting with Docker or you’re an experienced professional, WhaleDeck helps you work more efficiently.

Easily monitor your entire environment, gain insights into containers, networks, and volumes, and quickly address issues as they arise. WhaleDeck’s intuitive interface puts you in control, letting you tailor the tool to fit your workflow.

Start for free to:
· Add your Linux server and instantly visualize CPU, memory, drive, and network usage
· Access container logs, details, and insights to quickly identify and resolve issues
· Take direct action on containers – start, stop, or pause them individually or in bulk
· Use Split View to see multiple parts of your environment side by side
· Secure your setup with biometric authentication

Upgrade to WhaleDeck Pro to:
· Manage unlimited servers and maintain all your environments in one place
· Gain deeper insights into container resource usage
· Use iCloud syncing for seamless cross-device access
· Open server and container shells and run commands in the built-in terminal
· Enhance your workflow with home and lock screen widgets, plus custom refresh rates
· Dive deeper with detailed network and volume information

WhaleDeck Pro is available as a monthly or annual subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

For feedback or help, contact support@whaledeck.app or use the built-in form.
