## Product Overview

The all-in-one health and nutrition app. Track calories with AI, fast smarter, plan your meals, log supplements, journal your progress, and measure results - all in one place. Smart Goals automatically adjust your daily targets based on how you're performing, so you can stop obsessing over numbers and focus on showing up. The only thing between you and your goals is consistency, and Nutrify is built to keep you going. Just snap a photo and get instant calorie, protein, carb and fat breakdowns for any meal.

Nutrify is a paid subscription app. There is no free version, and the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial you can cancel anytime.

HOW IT WORKS

Take a photo of your food and get an instant calorie and macro breakdown. That's it. Nutrify identifies every item on your plate and gives you accurate nutrition data in seconds, not minutes.

Five ways to log your food:
  - Snap a photo (AI does the rest)
  - Scan a barcode for packaged foods
  - Say it to Siri
  - Type a quick description
  - Tap a saved meal for one-second logging

RECIPE IMPORT

Paste any recipe URL and get per-serving nutrition instantly. Or photograph a recipe from a cookbook and let AI extract the ingredients. Save everything to your personal library, or browse our curated recipes by diet.

MEAL PLANNING

Plan the days ahead instead of deciding at every meal:

  - A slot for every meal, on any day you choose, by week or by month
  - Build it from saved foods, your own recipes, or our curated ones
  - Repeat a meal, repeat a slot, or copy a whole day forward
  - Log today's planned meals in a single tap
  - Turn any range of days into a grocery list, grouped by aisle

BUILT FOR YOUR GOALS

Whether you're in a calorie deficit, building muscle, managing blood sugar, or on a GLP-1 medication plan, Nutrify adapts to you:

  - Smart Goals that adapt your daily target based on activity and weekly progress
  - Custom calorie and macro targets for your goal
  - GLP-1 support with research-backed protein ratios (works with Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro)
  - Sugar and carb tracking for diabetes management
  - Safe calorie floors to prevent under-eating

INTERMITTENT FASTING

Built-in fasting timer with popular plans: 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, and custom schedules. Track fasting phases, get eating window notifications, and review your fasting history.

SHOP SMARTER

Open Shop Mode at the grocery store. Scan any product before you buy it and get an instant verdict: does this fit your diet? See ingredient breakdowns, health scores, and smarter alternatives right in the aisle.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS

  - Daily, weekly, and monthly dashboards
  - Weight tracking with trend analysis
  - Calorie heatmap to spot patterns
  - Macro balance charts
  - Shareable progress snapshots

BEYOND CALORIES

  - Health Journal: progress photos, voice memos, and daily notes
  - Supplement Tracker: vitamins, medications, and GLP-1 injections with reminders
  - Body Measurements: 13+ metrics with trend charts and progress photos
  - Water Tracker: log water and 7 drink types with daily goals
  - Custom Nutrients: track any vitamin, mineral, or nutrient that matters to you

APPLE ECOSYSTEM

  - Lock Screen widgets for at-a-glance progress
  - Live Activities while logging meals
  - Apple Watch companion app
  - Apple Health sync for a complete picture

STAY CONSISTENT

Daily streaks, achievement milestones, and a design that makes tracking something you actually want to do. Nutrify fits into your day instead of interrupting it.

SUBSCRIPTION

  - Subscription auto-renews unless cancelled 24 hours before period ends
  - Manage subscriptions in Account Settings
