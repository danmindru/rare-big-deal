## Product Overview

KeyEcho is a free, open-source native desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux that plays recorded mechanical-keyboard sounds the instant you press a key. Audition every free pack and Crafted Pack in your browser first, then install the lightweight app with no bundled Electron browser via direct download, Homebrew, winget, or the Microsoft Store. Keystrokes fire locally on your machine with zero typing telemetry, giving any laptop or office keyboard a realistic thocky, creamy, or clicky sound. The core app and free packs need no account or key, while optional Crafted Packs are $3.99 each or $9.99 for All Sounds Forever (one-time, no subscription, offline key, 14-day refund).
