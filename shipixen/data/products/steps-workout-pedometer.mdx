## Product Overview

Steps is your complete fitness companion that turns every step into progress. Track steps, compete on the global leaderboard, block distracting apps until you hit your goal, and visualize your journey with beautiful charts and widgets.

AS SEEN AT WWDC 2026
Steps is featured in "Appreciation," the music video starring Erick the Architect, shown during Apple's WWDC 2026.

STEP TRACKING & GOALS
- Precise step counting powered by Apple HealthKit
- Customizable daily step goals with visual progress
- Streak tracking with rest days — never lose progress unfairly
- Goal achievement notifications and celebrations

AI COACH — CHAT WITH YOUR WORKOUTS
- Ask anything about your workouts and get personalized AI feedback
- Runs fully on device — your health data never leaves your iPhone
- Smart suggestions to help you train better and recover smarter

APP LOCK — STAY FOCUSED
- Block distracting apps until you reach your daily step goal
- Powered by Screen Time — apps auto-unlock when you hit your target
- The perfect motivation to get moving every day

STEPBOARD — GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
- Compete with walkers worldwide on the daily step leaderboard
- See your rank update in real-time as you walk
- Join or leave anytime — your choice

23+ WORKOUT TYPES
- Running, Walking, Cycling, Swimming, Hiking, HIIT, Yoga, and more
- Live tracking with real-time pace, distance, heart rate, and elevation
- Interactive route maps with GPS and GPX export
- 3D Route Playback — replay any run or ride as a cinematic 3D flyover
- Share workouts with customizable snapshot styles

BEAUTIFUL VISUALIZATIONS
- Hourly, weekly, and monthly interactive charts
- Calendar heatmaps to explore your activity history
- 8 color themes to personalize your experience
- Weather-integrated home dashboard

YEARLY INSIGHTS & RECORDS
- Year-end recap with personal bests and milestones
- 8 personal records: fastest pace, longest distance, most calories, and more
- Creative formats: yearly receipts, passport stamps
- Workout breakdown by type

APPLE WATCH & WIDGETS
- Native Watch app with real-time step tracking
- 3 watch complications for instant step counts
- 8 home screen widgets: steps, charts, calendar, streak, workouts
- Live Activity during active workouts

PERSONALIZATION
- Multiple custom app icons
- Light, dark, and system appearance modes
- Metric, imperial, or nautical units
- Configurable daily reminders

PRIVACY FIRST
- Health data stays on your device
- No ads, no selling your data
- Transparent privacy controls

Steps Pro unlocks the full experience with a free trial: widgets, Watch app, route maps, 3D Route Playback, AI Coach, advanced analytics, App Lock, yearly insights, and all color themes.
