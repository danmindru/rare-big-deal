## Product Overview

SelfOS brings your whole day into one calm place — self-care and productivity, together at last.

A BONSAI THAT GROWS WITH YOU

Plant your tree on day one. Every day you show up — a glass of water, a completed task, a good night's sleep — helps it grow.

ON YOUR WRIST

SelfOS for Apple Watch: your bonsai and streak, today's tasks and habits to tick, water with a +1 glass button, nutrition, activity and sleep at a glance. Watch face complications keep your day close. The watch app installs together with SelfOS on your iPhone.

MADE FOR iPAD AND iPHONE DUO

A layout that uses the room: dashboards side by side and two columns in landscape, larger water and sleep views.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN

Widgets for your bonsai with its streak, water with a +1 glass button, today's meals, sleep, activity, a Today list where you tick tasks and habits right on the screen, and a custom widget you build from the blocks you need.

YOUR HEALTH, IN FULL

- Watch and tracker sync — sleep with stages, workouts and steps arrive on their own through Apple Health
- Water — one-tap glasses, daily goal, gentle reminders
- Nutrition — calories and nutrients, barcode scanner, your own dishes and favorites, a fair calorie goal that counts your steps and workouts (food search powered by FatSecret)
- Sleep — duration and stages, a sleep score, night heart rate, HRV, breathing and blood oxygen, notes and trends
- Activity — steps, workouts, treadmill run and walk, active minutes

YOUR DAY, IN ORDER

- Daily tasks with subtasks, time blocks and repeats
- Goals with steps, deadlines and photo covers
- Calendar import — events from your phone become daily tasks, up to 30 days ahead with auto-refresh
- Habit categories with daily check-ins and schedules
- Shopping lists, always at hand

SEE THE WHOLE STORY

- Weekly and monthly stats for every module
- Calendar: open any date and see the full picture of that day
- Full Data Report and data export

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your data lives on your phone. No account required, no ads, no tracking. Health data never goes to us or to any analytics and stays on your devices: the summary of your day goes from your iPhone straight to your watch. Anonymous crash and usage statistics only — and you can turn that off with one switch. Before changing phones, save a backup file in Profile → Export data.

FREE AND PREMIUM

Free forever: all 8 modules with generous limits, the full bonsai story and all widgets. Premium unlocks unlimited tasks, goals, habits and lists, repeating tasks, sleep details with the sleep score, detailed nutrients, photo backgrounds, calendar import on autopilot, health widgets in the calendar, data export and more. 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. Monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase — subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled in Settings.

Care first — focus follows.
