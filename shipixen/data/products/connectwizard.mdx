## Product Overview

Access hidden App Store Connect insights about how your users interact with your app, both inside and outside. Build custom dashboards to track the metrics that matter most. Understand your user base better than ever before. No third-party SDK required.

HIDDEN DATA
Access App Store Connect analytics data that was never available before. Learn if your users engage with your privacy labels, reviews, and developer page. Analyze what share of users use the framework features you implemented and how they interact with them.

CUSTOM REPORTS
Build custom reports for your individual needs. Select from a wide variety of available reports and filter, categorize, and aggregate the data. Add visual overlays like prior period comparisons and save your custom presets for easy access to the data that matters to you.

NO SETUP REQUIRED
All data is automatically collected through App Store Connect. No third-party package or code changes needed. App Store Connect automatically collects aggregated data about user interactions outside and inside the app. Outside the app covers App Store pages and on device interactions like Shortcuts, widgets, and Live Activities. Inside the app, data like session metrics and framework usage statistics is available. Some data may not be available for apps with too few daily active users to protect individual privacy.
