## Product Overview

Buy it once, no ads, no subscription. Sleep Sounds is a mixer of 36 seamlessly looping nature and ambient sounds to listen to as you fall asleep, study, or take a well-earned timeout from your day. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

MIX YOUR OWN SOUNDSCAPES
Combine any sounds, set the volume of each one, and save your creations under My Mixes. Light rain with a distant campfire? Ocean waves with windchimes? It's up to you.

MADE FOR SLEEP
- Sleep timer from 10 minutes to 4 hours, or let it play all night.
- Every sound is balanced to the same comfortable loudness and fades in gently when it starts.
- Keeps playing when you lock your device or switch to other apps.
- Play and pause from the lock screen, Control Center or your headphones.
- Adjust the sound's volume inside the app without changing your device volume, so you can still hear alarms, alerts and phone calls.

EASY TO LIVE WITH
- Mark your favorites with a heart and find any sound with search.
- Full-screen artwork and a glowing LED level meter for each channel. Hide either in Settings.
- Favorites, settings and mixes stay in sync across your devices with iCloud.
- One purchase works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
