## Product Overview

TabLinker is a tab manager and link organizer for iPhone and iPad. Save links and whole Safari sessions in seconds, keep them in clean folders, and find them again on any of your devices.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
- Save every open Safari tab at once with the TabLinker Safari extension, without leaving Safari
- Reopen a saved folder's tabs right from the extension
- Save a link from the share sheet in any app, with the real page title, even for YouTube
- Paste a list of URLs, or import bookmark files (JSON and HTML)
- Shortcuts and Siri actions to save links and create folders

STAY ORGANIZED
- Flat folders with icons and colors, plus tags and notes
- Pin favorite tabs and folders for quick access
- Search titles, URLs, tags and notes
- Select many links at once to move, delete or refresh their titles
- Links you've opened are marked automatically
- Smart duplicate handling
- Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
- Private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, with no account
- On Mac, TabLinker also imports tabs from 13 browsers, restores whole windows, and can back up your open windows automatically

EXPORT IN YOUR FORMAT
JSON, HTML, Markdown, Plain Text, RTF, CSV, bullet lists and your own templates.

PRIVACY FIRST
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your links sync through your own iCloud.


TABLINKER PRO
- One-time unlock
- Unlimited saved tabs and links
- No subscription
- Works across all your Apple devices
