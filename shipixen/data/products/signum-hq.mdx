## Product Overview

Follow US stocks the easy way. SIGNUM HQ shows live prices for your stocks, explains every move in one line with AI, and tells you what matters today. Free.

- Your stocks, live: real-time prices during regular market hours, plus pre-market and after-hours moves
- AI in one line: why each of your stocks is moving, in plain words
- Daily AI briefings: a morning brief before the open and a closing report after the bell

WHAT YOU GET
- Market overview: major US indexes, futures, VIX, a sector heatmap and today's top movers
- AI morning brief and closing report: what happened, why, and what to watch next
- Earnings calendar: report dates, the expected move before the report (priced from options) and the actual move after
- News pulse: the headlines moving the market, summarized
- Push alerts and home-screen widgets
- For deeper research: money flow, dark-pool activity and options-based market indicators
- English · 日本語 · 한국어

From your first share to trading every day, SIGNUM HQ turns market data into a clear read in minutes. No account needed — just open it.
