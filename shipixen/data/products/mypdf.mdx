## Product Overview

Edit PDF text, sign documents and fill out PDF forms on iPhone and iPad.
Merge, compress, split, convert and scan to PDF in the same app.

myPDF is a PDF editor for the everyday stuff: the lease you have to sign tonight, the form that wants your address in tiny boxes, the scan that's too big to email.

EDIT THE PDF ITSELF
- Edit text: tap a line, fix the typo or the date, done
- Edit images: move, resize or replace a photo on the page
- Add pages from Files, Photos or a new scan
- Reorder, rotate, duplicate, extract or delete pages
- Watermark, plus Header & footer with page numbers: two tools, each with a live preview

SIGN AND FILL
- Draw your signature once or take it from a photo, then reuse it
- Fill out PDF forms: text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons and lists
- Add text anywhere on the page
- Sign it and send it back, no printer needed

SCAN TO PDF
- Scan documents, receipts and notes with edge detection, then crop, rotate and filter each page
- Save as a searchable PDF (OCR), so you can find and copy the text later
- Clean up shadows on scans
- Turn photos into a PDF

MERGE, SPLIT, COMPRESS, CONVERT
- Merge PDF files and pick the pages you need from each one
- Split a PDF into ranges or single pages
- Compress a PDF that's too big to email, with the text still selectable
- Word, PowerPoint, Excel and EPUB to PDF
- PDF to Word, PowerPoint, EPUB, plain text or RTF

PRIVATE DETAILS
- Redact: mark an area or search for a name or number.
- Passwords: lock a PDF with an open password, and add an owner password that limits printing and editing
- Sanitize: remove hidden metadata, scripts and attachments
- Secure Wallet: keep private PDFs behind Face ID or a PIN

READ AND MARK UP
- Highlight, underline, strike out, draw with your finger or Apple Pencil, add notes, stamps and links
- Search, bookmarks, table of contents and folders
- Night and Sepia reading modes, and the screen stays on while you read

CHAT WITH YOUR PDF
Ask a long contract or manual a question and get the answer from its own text, with the page it came from. Tap it to jump there.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Signatures are drawn or taken from a photo. They are not certificate-based.
- Edit text works on PDFs that have real text. Scanned pages and some fonts can't be edited, and myPDF tells you when that's the case.
- Chat uses Apple Intelligence where it's available. Otherwise it needs a one-time download of about 1.6 GB, then it runs on your device.
- Password-protected PDFs work in the tools. Compressed and greyscale copies keep the password, split and merged files are saved without one.
