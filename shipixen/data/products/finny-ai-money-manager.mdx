## Product Overview

Payments that log themselves. Pay with Apple Pay, get a payment alert, or show Finny your screen, and Finny logs it for you. Or snap a receipt, say it, or type it. Every currency adds up to one total in your home currency, and Finny never asks for your bank login.

TAP TO TRACK
Use Apple Pay like you always do. Finny automatically detects each purchase and logs it for you: amount, merchant, date, all done. Set it up once in Settings.

SCREEN LOG
Some cards never announce a purchase. Show Finny the screen instead: your banking app, an order confirmation, a receipt. One press reads it and logs it. Works with every card, and never needs your bank login.

NOTIFICATION LOG (PRO)
Payment alerts from any app log themselves: your bank, your wallet, even a train fare. Set it up once in Settings. Needs iOS 27.

HOUSEHOLDS (PRO)
Track money with up to 5 other people. Share only the entries you choose. History shows who logged what. One family plan covers everyone.

SMART SEARCH
Search History the way you'd say it, like "dining out last month", and see the total and where it went. Questions use Apple Intelligence, on your device.

HASHTAGS (PRO)
Tag entries like #Europe or #Wedding, then search the tag to see what a whole trip or event cost, across every category.

BUDGETS
Set monthly limits for any group of categories and see at a glance what you have left to spend. Turn on rollover and your leftover (or overspend) carries into next month, so you stay honest with yourself. Budget in any currency, independent of your home currency.

WIDGETS & QUICK LOG
Widgets for your Home and Lock Screen show your month, a budget, and your spending trend at a glance. Quick Log files an expense in seconds without opening Finny, from Spotlight, Siri, the Action Button, or Back Tap. No AI, free for everyone.

RECURRING TRANSACTIONS
Set up recurring rules for rent, subscriptions, salary, or any transaction that repeats. Choose daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Finny automatically logs each occurrence for you, sends reminders before they're due, and shows upcoming entries on your dashboard.

PHOTOS & RECEIPTS (PRO)
Attach a photo to any entry. It stays on your device and your own iCloud, never on our servers.

TRACK MONEY YOUR WAY
- Scan receipts with your camera, or share them from any app, and Finny captures amounts, merchants, and dates.
- Use your voice to say what you spent or earned and Finny records it for you.
- Use natural text like "coffee $5" or "salary $2500" and let the AI fill in the rest.

SEE YOUR FINANCES CLEARLY
- View income, expenses, and net balance with clean, easy-to-read charts.
- Compare current spending against last period with side-by-side charts.
- Understand where your money goes with category breakdowns and monthly trends.

BUILT FOR A GLOBAL LIFESTYLE
Finny supports over 150 currencies with automatic exchange rates. Log transactions in any currency and view everything in your home currency with no manual conversion. Ideal for travelers, freelancers, and remote workers.

CUSTOMIZE IT TO FIT YOUR LIFE
Create your own categories. Add payment methods like bank accounts, cards, and cash. Start your month on payday (Pro). Make Finny match how you actually manage money.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your device. Use Face ID or a PIN to protect your finances. Optional cloud backup keeps your data synced across devices. No account required to get started.

FINNY PRO
New subscribers start with a free three-day trial. Pro unlocks Tap to Track, Screen Log, Notification Log, Households, Hashtags, receipt scanning, voice and text input, photo attachments, and Cloud Backup. One family plan covers up to 6 people. Every new user gets one free AI request to try before subscribing.
