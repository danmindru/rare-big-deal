## Product Overview

Stop typing every transaction. Finny lets you track your money by snapping receipts, speaking naturally, or typing simple phrases. The built-in AI handles the details so you can stay focused on your life instead of spreadsheets.

TAP TO TRACK
Use Apple Pay like you always do. Finny automatically detects each purchase and logs it for you: amount, merchant, date, all done. Set it up once in Settings, then never manually log an Apple Pay transaction again.

SCREEN LOG
Some cards never announce a purchase. Show Finny the screen instead: your banking app, an order confirmation, a receipt. One press reads it and logs it. Works with every card, and never needs your bank login.

BUDGETS
Set monthly limits for any group of categories and see at a glance what you have left to spend. Group Coffee and Eating Out into one limit, or carve out a separate envelope for Travel. Turn on rollover and your leftover (or overspend) carries into next month, so you stay honest with yourself. Budget in any currency, independent of your home currency.

WIDGETS & QUICK LOG
See the month's spending at a glance with the new Spending Calendar and Money Wisdom widgets on your Home and Lock Screen. Quick Log files an expense in seconds without opening Finny, from Spotlight, Siri, the Action Button, or Back Tap. No AI, free for everyone.

RECURRING TRANSACTIONS
Set up recurring rules for rent, subscriptions, salary, or any transaction that repeats. Choose daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Finny automatically logs each occurrence for you, sends reminders before they're due, and shows upcoming entries on your dashboard. Edit one, edit all, or just this one: you're in control.

SCAN RECEIPTS FROM ANY APP
Share a receipt from Photos, Safari, your camera roll, or any app: Finny's Share Extension scans it instantly without switching apps. Select up to 5 images and process them all at once.

PHOTOS & RECEIPTS (PRO)
Attach a photo to any entry. It stays on your device and your own iCloud, never on our servers.

TRACK MONEY YOUR WAY
- Use your camera to scan receipts and automatically capture amounts, merchants, and dates.
- Use your voice to say what you spent or earned and Finny records it for you.
- Use natural text like "coffee $5" or "salary $2500" and let the AI fill in the rest.
- Scan banking or e-wallet screenshots to batch import multiple transactions.

SEE YOUR FINANCES CLEARLY
- Track monthly budgets with progress bars and rollover at a glance.
- View income, expenses, and net balance with clean, easy-to-read charts.
- Compare current spending against last period with side-by-side charts.
- Average daily spending line helps you stay on track.
- Understand where your money goes with category breakdowns and monthly trends.
- Track upcoming recurring bills and subscriptions from your dashboard.
- Search and filter your full transaction history anytime.

BUILT FOR A GLOBAL LIFESTYLE
Finny supports over 150 currencies with automatic exchange rates. Log transactions in any currency and view everything in your home currency with no manual conversion. Ideal for travelers, freelancers, and remote workers.

CUSTOMIZE IT TO FIT YOUR LIFE
Create your own categories. Add payment methods like bank accounts, cards, and cash. Safely reassign entries when removing a category or payment method: nothing gets lost. Make Finny match how you actually manage money.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your device. Use Face ID or a PIN to protect your finances. Optional cloud backup keeps your data synced across devices. No account required to get started.

FINNY PRO
Unlock AI-powered features with a free three-day trial, including Tap to Track, receipt scanning, voice input, and natural language logging. Every new user gets one free AI request to try before subscribing.
