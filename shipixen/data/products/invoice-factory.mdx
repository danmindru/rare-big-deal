## Product Overview

Invoice Factory is the all-in-one invoice maker and bookkeeping app for freelancers, self-employed professionals and small business owners. Create professional invoices in seconds, track payments and bills, and see exactly where your business stands, right from your iPhone or iPad.

CREATE INVOICES IN SECONDS
- Pick a customer, add saved items, and let taxes, discounts and shipping calculate automatically
- Share invoices as PDFs via Mail, Messages or AirDrop, or download and print them
- Add your signature and attach files to invoices and bills
- Dashboard quick actions get you to a new invoice fast

60+ PROFESSIONAL TEMPLATES
- Six styles: Minimal, Modern, Bold, Elegant, Classic and Creative
- Add your logo, signature, accent colour and your own terms & conditions
- Preview every template with your actual invoice before you choose

DESIGNED FOR IPHONE DUO
- Unfold for a two-pane workspace: fill in an invoice on one side and watch the PDF update live on the other, or keep your charts in view
- Fold or unfold mid-task with no reloads and no lost typing

KNOW WHERE YOUR BUSINESS STANDS
- Dashboard with your overall balance and five charts: Outstanding, Invoice & Bill Status, Profit or Loss, Cash Flow and Revenue
- View any period, from the last 7 days to all time, or pick custom dates
- Six Home Screen widgets in medium and large sizes, each with its own date range

BILLS, PAYMENTS & ACCOUNTS
- Record full or partial payments on invoices and bills
- Account balances update automatically
- See paid, due and overdue status at a glance

CUSTOMERS & SUPPLIERS
- Keep contact details, invoices, bills and payment history in one place
- See who owes you, and who you owe
- Export customer and supplier ledgers and transaction reports as PDF

BUILT FOR HOW YOU WORK
- Run multiple businesses, each with its own customers, items, taxes, accounts and terms, and switch with one tap
- Invoice, take payments and track balances in multiple currencies
- Sign in with Apple or Google for encrypted cloud sync across iPhone and iPad, or use the app without an account
- Import and export full backups
- Native design with Liquid Glass, colour themes, and light and dark mode
- Available in 19 languages, following your device's language and region

Upgrade to Plus for unlimited invoices, bills, customers and suppliers, multiple businesses, signatures, attachments, sign-in and sync, import/export, and app themes. Choose Premium for everything in Plus, plus all 60+ premium templates.
